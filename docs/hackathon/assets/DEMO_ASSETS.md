# Demo asset notes

## `demo.mp4` and `demo-poster.png`

The selected 25-second voiced release cut shows a synthetic £200 London receipt uploaded and manually reviewed in the app, followed by USDC share review, separate reimbursement requests, a mobile Zcash demo checkout, simulated payment success and organizer progress. This uploaded GBP receipt is not the app's built-in synthetic 200 AED sample described in the root README's local demo steps. The video is product footage and graphics; the bill, receipt and payment status are synthetic. The checkout uses demo controls. No live quote is requested or executed, no wallet signs or transfers funds, and no payment settles.
