## Choosing our scope

Score candidate ideas on a real user's problem, a concrete reason to use NEAR, an integration we can prove early, one complete demo journey, and feasibility before the deadline. Prefer a core journey we can demonstrate in roughly two minutes. These are project planning criteria, not organizer scoring weights.

Before committing to an integration, verify data/API access, credentials, costs, network support and what can be demonstrated without real money. The starter prompts target a full two-week build; our remaining time is shorter.

Do not choose extra tracks just because they are available. Explain how the implemented behavior fits each selected track in the eventual submission README.
